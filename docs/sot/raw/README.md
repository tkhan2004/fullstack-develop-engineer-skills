# raw/ — local only

Put your own copies of books, PDFs, course material and scans here.

**Nothing in this directory is committed.** It is gitignored at the `docs/sot/.gitignore`
level. Do not force-add files from here.

Suggested local organisation:

```text
raw/
├── books/
│   ├── clean-code.pdf
│   ├── a-philosophy-of-software-design.pdf
│   └── designing-data-intensive-applications.pdf
├── courses/
└── talks/
```

What *is* committed: your distilled notes in `../notes/`, written in your own words with
citations. See [../README.md](../README.md) §1.
